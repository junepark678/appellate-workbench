# United States Court of Appeals for the Fourth Circuit

## Piedmont Booksellers Guild v. City of Norvale

No. SYN-CA4-26-CV-4103-CF-NEVER-OCCURRED

Adverse Mandate

Issued June 12, 2026

The judgment entered May 22, 2026, is transmitted as the mandate of this Court. Exercising interlocutory jurisdiction under 28 U.S.C. § 1292(a)(1), the Court denied dismissal of the recurring-events controversy as moot, reversed the district court's grant of preliminary relief, and remanded the matter for further proceedings consistent with the accompanying opinion. The Court denied the separate request to preserve preliminary entitlement through a limited corrective remand under Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 65(c) and (d).

The reversal concerns preliminary relief on the record developed for the January hearing. It does not constitute a final adjudication of the Guild's facial or as-applied First Amendment claims, direct entry of judgment against the Guild on the complaint, or foreclose consideration of later evidence in the district court. The district court shall conduct further proceedings within the scope of the opinion and may address requests properly presented after remand.

The allocation of appellate costs is governed by the certified judgment accompanying this mandate. A copy of the May 22 opinion is transmitted with that judgment as required by Federal Rule of Appellate Procedure 41(a).
