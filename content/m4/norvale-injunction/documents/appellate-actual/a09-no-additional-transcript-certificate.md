This certificate concerns transcript preparation only. If the Court identifies an omitted reported proceeding, counsel will respond promptly and will not treat this filing as permission to withhold a required transcript.

Counsel for the Guild was consulted regarding transcript status and did not identify an additional proceeding that should be ordered as of February 9. This statement does not bind the Guild to the City's proposed issues or prevent a timely motion to supplement the record if a genuine omission is discovered. It confirms only that the existing January 19 transcript is the full reported proceeding presently necessary for review.
