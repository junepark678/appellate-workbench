> **SYNTHETIC APPELLATE MANDATE — FICTIONAL CASE — NOT AN ACTUAL MANDATE**

# United States Court of Appeals for the Fourth Circuit

## Piedmont Booksellers Guild v. City of Norvale

No. SYN-CA4-26-CV-4103
Mandate
Issued July 9, 2026

The judgment of this Court entered June 18, 2026, is now effective through issuance of this mandate. A certified copy of the panel opinion and judgment accompanies this instrument. The Court exercised jurisdiction under 28 U.S.C. § 1292(a)(1), denied the City's mootness request, affirmed the Guild's entitlement to preliminary protection, and remanded only for correction of the preliminary order under Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 65(c) and 65(d).

The district court shall conduct proceedings consistent with the opinion. The January 23 preliminary injunction remains effective until a superseding compliant order is entered. This mandate does not dissolve interim protection, resolve the complaint finally, or restrict the district court's authority to address materially changed circumstances on a proper record. Each side shall bear its own appellate costs.
