## Evidentiary agreements and reserved rulings

Counsel shall describe it as a rendering rather than a machine-generated legal or product-disposition decision. The assay and lot-disposition materials may be presented with proprietary technical detail generalized. Counsel shall request a sidebar before mentioning an item subject to a reserved ruling. No lawyer may imply that a reserved objection has been decided favorably.

## Daily sequence and courtroom procedure

Jury selection and preliminary instructions begin at 9:00 on October 6. Openings will be limited to forty-five minutes per side. The Court will provide ordinary breaks and a lunch recess, with witnesses directed not to discuss unfinished testimony.

Counsel shall provide the clerk the next day’s witness order before adjournment. Questions should identify documents by trial exhibit number and descriptive name. The reporter will mark each logical transcript division through page breaks in the source without printing civil-record or appellate labels inside the testimony.

At the close of a party’s proof, legal motions will be heard outside the jury’s presence. The moving party must specify the issue and evidentiary deficiency asserted. Neither the pretrial submissions nor a general reservation will add a ground that the motion does not actually state.

The final charge conference will occur before closing on October 14. Objections must be stated distinctly after the proposed charge is reviewed. The Court anticipates separate verdict questions for liability, causation, gross damages, mitigation, and net award.
