# Blue Ember's Notice of Appeal

## Filed January 12, 2026

United States District Court for the Western District of North Carolina
Blue Ember Biologics, LLC v. Granite Heron Logistics, Inc.
Civil Action No. SYN-WDNC-24-CV-0520

Blue Ember Biologics, LLC appeals to the United States Court of Appeals for the Fourth Circuit from the final amended judgment and posttrial order entered December 15, 2025. The appeal challenges the grant of judgment as a matter of law on mitigation, the resulting change from a 3.9-million-dollar judgment to 2.7 million dollars, and all rulings merged into that final disposition that are properly reviewable.

Blue Ember also seeks review of the conditional Rule 59 new-trial determination and its damages-only scope to the extent necessary under Rule 50(c). The notice does not appeal the favorable liability or causation findings, does not create a second district judgment, and does not assert that a different pre-verdict motion occurred.

The November 10 Rule 50(b) and Rule 59 motion was timely and was disposed of December 15. This January 12 notice follows within the restarted civil appeal period. Blue Ember requests transmission of the actual district record and assignment of the appellate docket through ordinary clerk procedure.
