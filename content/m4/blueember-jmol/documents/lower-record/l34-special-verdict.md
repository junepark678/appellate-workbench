## Gross damages and mitigation answers

Its itemized answers were 1.35 million dollars for the cost embodied in the rejected validation lot, 850,000 dollars for incremental replacement and associated expense, and two million dollars for bounded delay loss. The clerk verified that these three figures sum to the stated gross amount.

On Granite Heron’s affirmative defense, the jury found that Blue Ember failed to take every reasonable step to reduce part of the caused delay. It fixed the net mitigation deduction at 300,000 dollars after considering the genuine Cobalt Finch capacity, unfinished BE-214 qualification, estimated alternative expense, compressed decision window, execution risk, and expected schedule benefit.

The form requested a net figure, not gross avoidance. Jurors were instructed to subtract reasonable incremental alternative cost before stating the deduction and to avoid reducing original-lot value merely because earlier replacement might shorten delay. The final arithmetic directed by the form is 4.2 million dollars less 300,000 dollars, producing 3.9 million dollars.
