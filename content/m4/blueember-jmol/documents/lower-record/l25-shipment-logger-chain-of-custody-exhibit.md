The readable curve derives from the preserved CTX9-44721 sampling sequence without interpolation across missing observations. It shows the payload rising above the minus-sixty upper boundary at 13:08. Warming continued to a recorded maximum of minus 42.8 degrees Celsius at 13:37. The boundary-to-boundary duration is fifty-four minutes. Later readings are displayed after the return so the recovery shape remains visible, but they are not averaged backward to recast the excursion. The report also retains the minus-eighty lower boundary and minus-seventy operating setpoint for context. No authorized grace period, rolling average, or single-reading exception appears in the shipment instructions.

The source export’s device identifier and clock setting match the origin and custody records. The parties stipulated that the plotted time and temperature values may be considered as recorded. They did not stipulate that the graph proves universal inactivity, validation suitability, negligence, foreseeability, or a particular monetary loss. Those questions require the other admitted evidence and the Court’s instructions.

## Integrity statement and evidentiary limits

The Granite Heron custodian compared this presentation with the retained custody record, device export, controller information, route file, and contact log. The plotted values preserve sampling order; the chronology identifies approximations where a witness or later investigation supplied the source. No correction overwrites an original timestamp. Supplemental explanations remain separately dated.

The event ended before the trailer left Fletcher, and delivery occurred after the monitor had returned within range. Those facts may be considered with the magnitude, duration, intended validation use, assay material, and September 16 disposition. They do not compel a finding that receiving caused the warming or that recovery conclusively restored suitability.

Granite Heron preserved the source export so a reader can distinguish recorded temperature from advocate characterization. The rendering is not raw device software, a stability study, or a machine interpretation of BE-214. Admission likewise does not resolve whether the excursion caused all claimed consequences. The custodian certified only that the collected records are the business and device records used to reconstruct shipment GHL-ASH-091224-087.
