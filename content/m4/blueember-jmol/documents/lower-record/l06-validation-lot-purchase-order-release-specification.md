## Sampling, testing, and stability assessment

After a reported excursion, Blue Ember may request representative sampling and confirmatory testing. Samples must remain traceable to the lot and be handled under a protocol that avoids adding a second uncontrolled exposure. The laboratory will record method, analyst, instrument, date, result, and any deviation.

Stability assessment considers magnitude, duration, thermal recovery, packaging configuration, available development data, and the planned validation role. A passing activity result may show that tested samples retain a measured function. It may not establish uniform condition across all vials or reconstruct documentation that the validation protocol required.

Conversely, the quality reviewer shall not claim total biochemical failure when testing does not support that conclusion. The disposition rationale should identify whether rejection rests on measured inactivity, uncertainty, failure of a validation prerequisite, or a combination. Scientific precision is required even when the ultimate decision is rejection.

Granite Heron may receive a sufficient summary for claim evaluation, subject to protection of formulation and method details.
