## Damages and offset computations

Blue Ember’s current gross computation is 4.2 million dollars. It allocates 1.35 million to cost embodied in the rejected validation lot, 850,000 to replacement production and directly associated services, and 2 million to a defined period of delay-related contribution and milestone loss. It reserves refinement based on discovery but will not add a new category without timely supplementation.

Granite Heron disputes the foundation, causation, certainty, foreseeability, and contractual recoverability of each component. For mitigation, it presently calculates that the December 2 Cobalt Finch slot could have prevented 1.8 million dollars of the claimed delay. It subtracts an estimated 300,000 dollars for transfer and qualification, producing a proposed net deduction of 1.5 million dollars.

Granite Heron’s offset model is alternative. It does not admit the gross 4.2 million dollars or concede that all remaining amounts are proper. Blue Ember disputes that the slot was a certain, qualified substitute and disputes the claimed avoidance period.

Both parties will produce calculation files in a reasonably usable form, identify assumptions and source records, and avoid double counting. Insurance, credits, reusable work, and ordinary costs will be addressed to the extent evidence shows they compensate or reduce the same asserted loss.
