## Mitigation calculation framework

A net mitigation calculation compares loss likely avoided with incremental cost and additional risk caused by the alternative. Granite Heron’s model begins with 1.8 million dollars in allegedly avoided delay and subtracts 300,000 dollars for transfer and qualification, yielding 1.5 million dollars.

That subtraction is mathematically correct if both inputs are accepted. The disputed premise is that a timely successful December campaign would avoid the full 1.8 million. The proposal contemplated December 2 manufacturing start, not immediate final validation release, and it contained readiness contingencies.

I constructed a transparent formula: net deduction equals the factfinder’s supported avoided-loss amount minus reasonable incremental alternative cost, floored at zero. The calculation should not subtract a separate risk penalty twice if risk has already reduced the avoided-loss input.

Likewise, the 300,000 dollars should not be added to Blue Ember’s actual gross damage because it was not incurred. It becomes relevant only when evaluating the hypothetical alternative. If the factfinder concludes no reasonable alternative was established, the gross 4.2 million remains my opinion. If it finds partial feasibility, the workpaper accepts a partial avoided-loss figure.
