Before Composite Jurist March, presiding, Composite Jurist Rowan, and Composite Jurist Slate. Granite Heron’s July 27 motion to stay mandate is granted subject to the conditions stated below. Blue Ember’s July 28 response confirms that its litigation committee authorized preparation of a petition for a writ of certiorari and supports a limited stay secured for the full affirmed judgment.

The contemplated petition presents a substantial question under Rule 41(d)(2)(A): whether express preservation of mitigation permitted the court to direct the entire $1,500,000 deduction when technical qualification, timing, execution, and successful substitute performance allegedly remained open to rational dispute. That question controlled the branch judgment and is genuinely presented by the party adversely affected by the mitigation ruling.

Granite Heron shall submit security covering $2,700,000 plus projected postjudgment interest for district-court approval within two business days. The undertaking must remain effective throughout the stay.

The clerk shall withhold mandate. This order does not change the July 9 opinion, the two-component affirmance, or any merits determination.

The stay begins upon entry and may not exceed ninety days unless Blue Ember files its petition or obtains an extension of the filing period for good cause. This timely order intervenes before mandate, so no recall is required. The clerk shall retain the mandate until a dissolution or other terminating event directs release.
