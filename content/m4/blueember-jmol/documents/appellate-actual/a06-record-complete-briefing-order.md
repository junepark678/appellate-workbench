## Tailored merits schedule

By direction of the court, and under its authority to adjust briefing time, the following exact schedule governs: Blue Ember’s opening brief and the parties’ appendix are due March 9, 2026; Granite Heron’s response brief is due April 8, 2026; and Blue Ember’s reply brief is due April 20, 2026. The opening period and reply period are shortened from ordinary default intervals to permit orderly consideration during the court’s spring calendar. These are court-set dates, not calculations counsel may extend by agreement.

A motion affecting a deadline must be filed before the date at issue and must state opposing counsel’s position, prior extensions, concrete reasons, and the requested replacement date. Unless an order grants relief, the existing deadline remains controlling. The response date falls thirty days after the opening filing; the accelerated reply is due twelve days after the response. Filing on the last day must be completed through the authorized system before its applicable cutoff.

Briefs shall address appellate jurisdiction, the actual content of both pre-verdict motions, the distinct standards governing renewed judgment and a Rule 59 new trial, the separability of damages from liability and causation, and the consequences of Rule 50(c). The schedule does not predetermine how those questions should be answered.
