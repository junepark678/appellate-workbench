# Minute entries and submission order

The panel asked Rusk whether dismissal of the media-based contention would leave a reviewable
clearly-established-law question. Counsel answered yes and requested reversal on that legal prong.
The panel asked Ellison whether affirmance would bind the trial factfinder to her account. Counsel
answered no and requested a judgment expressly preserving the competing histories.

Questions also addressed the significance of a single drive-stun cycle, the protective nature of
the seizure, *Armstrong*'s prospective notice, *Barricks*'s appellate framework, and *Zorn*'s
demand for specificity. Both parties agreed that *Barricks* and *Zorn* postdate the February 3,
2025 encounter and cannot themselves furnish notice to Rusk. They disagreed about whether the
older Fourth Circuit cases provided a sufficiently close rule.

The appeal was submitted at the conclusion of rebuttal. No supplemental brief is ordered.
Counsel must notify the Clerk if a material jurisdictional development occurs before decision.
The audio recording of argument will be maintained under the Court's ordinary access practice;
this minute sheet is not a transcript and does not amend any position stated in the filed briefs.
