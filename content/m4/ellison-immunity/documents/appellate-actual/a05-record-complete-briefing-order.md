# Record-Complete Notice and Briefing Order

That administrative determination means the designated district materials are available; it does
not decide whether a particular item is admissible, relevant, or within the scope of collateral-
order review.

2026. Appellee Mara Ellison shall file her response brief by April 15, 2026. Any reply brief shall
be filed by May 6, 2026. These dates are fixed from the present order and may be changed only by a
later court order.

include the notice of appeal, the order under review, relevant motion papers, the factual materials
needed to understand the assumed-facts analysis, and the district docket certificate. Neither the
briefs nor appendix may imply that an appellate page label was part of an original district
filing.

# Briefing instructions and disputed-fact boundary

The opening brief must begin with a statement of appellate jurisdiction and must identify which
arguments accept the district court's plaintiff-favorable facts. If Rusk asks the Court to reject
an account as inconsistent with recordings, he shall state that request separately and explain
the basis for interlocutory jurisdiction. Ellison may challenge jurisdiction over that portion
without filing a separate motion unless a rule otherwise requires.

Each brief shall distinguish a dispute about what occurred from the legal effect of facts assumed
for summary judgment. Descriptions of the body-camera interval from 19:53:08 through 19:53:22 must
honor the district record: Rusk's view is materially blocked, Vega's view does not reliably show
the hands, and synchronized audio does not independently establish a strike or equipment reach.
No party may present generated imagery as record evidence.

The joint appendix may include the authenticated transcript, frame-sequence map, occlusion
analysis, synchronized-audio comparison, and custody declaration. Inclusion does not settle the
parties' competing interpretations. Counsel shall confer on the index and certify that the
contents came from the district record.

The ordinary word, format, service, and disclosure requirements of the appellate rules remain in
effect.

# Administration of the appeal

No stay request is pending. The denial of summary judgment did not itself impose an affirmative
injunction, and this order does not direct the district court's management of matters outside the
appeal. Counsel shall notify the Clerk promptly of settlement, a material district-court event, a
related proceeding, or any development affecting appellate jurisdiction.

The Court has not selected an argument date. After briefing is complete, the appeal will be
screened under the ordinary decisional process. A later calendar notice will identify any panel,
session, time allocation, and submission instructions. Parties should not infer a merits view
from scheduling or record completion.

If counsel identifies a genuine omission from the certified record, the parties must confer and
propose a correction tied to a district filing or exhibit. A motion to supplement with material
created after January 9 must state an independent legal basis; mere usefulness on appeal is
insufficient. The Clerk will not receive native video through ordinary appendix filing without
advance coordination regarding format and custody.

Entered at the direction of the Court on February 2, 2026, with electronic notice to all counsel.
The filing dates stated above control unless modified by a subsequent order.
