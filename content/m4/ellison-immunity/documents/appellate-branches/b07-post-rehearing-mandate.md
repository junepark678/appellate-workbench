# United States Court of Appeals for the Fourth Circuit

## Mara Ellison v. Officer Nolan Rusk

No. SYN-CA4-26-CV-4104-CF-NEVER-OCCURRED

### Mandate Following Rehearing Denial

Issued August 11, 2026

To the United States District Court for the Western District of Virginia:

The judgment of this Court, entered June 30, 2026, is now effective. The factual-sufficiency portion of Officer Nolan Rusk's interlocutory appeal is dismissed for lack of collateral-order jurisdiction. The district court's January 9, 2026 legal denial of qualified immunity is reversed, and the action is remanded with instructions to enter summary judgment for Rusk on Mara Ellison's remaining individual-capacity damages claim.

This mandate follows the Court's August 4 denial of Ellison's timely combined petition for panel rehearing and rehearing en banc. It issues seven days after that denial under Federal Rule of Appellate Procedure 41(b). No mandate stay is in effect on this branch, and no earlier mandate issued.

The accompanying published opinion supplies the controlling reasons and boundaries. In particular, the Court did not decide whether Ellison struck Rusk, moved toward police equipment, or only pulled from a painful wrist grasp. Qualified immunity was granted while accepting the plaintiff-favorable version solely for legal analysis. The district court shall not treat the appellate disposition as a factual finding.

Appellant may recover allowable costs in the amount later fixed through ordinary clerk procedure.
