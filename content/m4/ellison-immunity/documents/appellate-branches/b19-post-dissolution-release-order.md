### Mandate Release Order After Dissolution of Limited Stay

Entered August 11, 2026

The mandate is released for issuance today. Officer Nolan Rusk obtained a limited stay on July 20 while he completed review of a possible certiorari petition. On August 10, he timely notified the Court that he had abandoned the planned petition, had not sought an extension, and requested dissolution. The panel dissolved the stay effective that day and specifically directed next-day issuance.

No event under Federal Rule of Appellate Procedure 41(d)(2) continues the stay. There is no filed certiorari petition and no granted extension of the petition period. The Court is not acting upon a Supreme Court denial; it is implementing its own dissolution order after voluntary abandonment.

The Clerk has confirmed that the June 30 opinion and judgment remain unchanged. The mandate will make effective the disposition dismissing Rusk's factual appellate target and affirming the legal denial of qualified immunity on Ellison's assumed facts. Historical questions hidden by the recordings remain unresolved for any necessary district proceedings.

Release on August 11 provides the exact transition ordered by the panel. The mandate did not issue automatically at midnight when the stay ended, and it did not remain withheld for a default seven-day interval. This administrative order records the authorized issuance event and directs electronic transmission to the district court.
