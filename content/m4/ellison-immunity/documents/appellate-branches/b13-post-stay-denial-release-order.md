# United States Court of Appeals for the Fourth Circuit

## Mara Ellison v. Officer Nolan Rusk

No. SYN-CA4-26-CV-4104-CF-NEVER-OCCURRED

### Mandate Release Order Following Stay Denial

Entered July 21, 2026

The mandate is released for issuance today. Officer Nolan Rusk moved on July 15 to stay the mandate pending a proposed petition for certiorari. The Court denied that motion on July 20 and, exercising its authority under Federal Rule of Appellate Procedure 41(b), expressly shortened the otherwise applicable post-denial interval to direct issuance on July 21.

The Clerk has verified that no later court order supersedes that direction and no stay remains effective. Today's release implements the schedule stated in the denial order. It is not based on an assumption that a denied motion leaves the original clock untouched; the panel's express shortening provides the legal basis for next-day issuance.

The mandate shall transmit the actual-route June 30 disposition: dismissal of Rusk's attempt to obtain appellate fact review and affirmance of the legal denial of qualified immunity on the supported facts assumed for Ellison. The district court may then resume proceedings on the remaining individual-capacity claim without treating the assumed account as an adjudicated version of events.

Release does not foreclose a certiorari petition or decide any application that may be presented elsewhere. It means only that this Court declined to retain control of the case and directed its judgment to take effect through mandate issuance.
