### Panel Order Dissolving the Stay

Upon consideration of Rusk's notice, the limited stay of mandate entered July 20 is DISSOLVED effective August 10, 2026. The sole stated purpose of the stay was to permit a defined period for final certiorari review and filing. Rusk's election not to proceed eliminates that predicate before any petition or extension could continue relief under Federal Rule of Appellate Procedure 41(d)(2).

This is not an order responding to a Supreme Court denial. Rule 41(d)(4)'s command concerning receipt of an order denying certiorari therefore does not apply. Nor did the stay simply lapse in silence; a timely abandonment notice supplied the event, and the panel affirmatively ends relief through this order.

The mandate shall not be treated as issued today. To provide a definite administrative sequence, the Court directs the Clerk to release and issue it on August 11, 2026. That next-day direction is authorized by Rule 41(b)'s power to set issuance timing and by the terms reserved in the grant order. No additional seven-day period follows dissolution of a granted stay whose terms and ending event control.

The June 30 judgment and opinion remain unchanged. Their disposition will become effective upon issuance tomorrow. The order neither resolves disputed movements during the camera obstruction nor alters the plaintiff-favorable assumptions governing the legal appeal.
