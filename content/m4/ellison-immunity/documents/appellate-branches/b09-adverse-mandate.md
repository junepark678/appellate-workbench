Issued July 21, 2026

No stay of mandate is pending or effective in this counterfactual route.

The opinion's assumed facts are not adjudicated historical facts. The appellate court did not decide whether Ellison struck Rusk, approached his duty belt, or instead only recoiled and pulled. It granted immunity because the law on February 3, 2025 did not clearly establish the specific assumed violation beyond debate. That distinction binds further proceedings.

Allowable appellate costs are awarded to Rusk, subject to taxation under governing procedures.
