## Clarification Questions

First, please confirm whether “final or superseded editions” means only the last approved copy of each version, rather than every duplicate distributed to a custodian. ORER proposes to treat byte-identical or content-identical copies as one responsive record and to retain a single authoritative copy for review. Distribution logs would remain independently responsive when they record approval or supersession.

Second, please state whether the request reaches final training editions and quality-review maps. Some Grid Sentinel records are approved for fictional tabletop exercises but are not designated for ordinary program use. ORER proposes to include a final exercise edition if it was effective during the date range, while excluding draft scenarios and participant notes.

Third, please confirm that correspondence is responsive only when it constitutes the final approval memorandum or supersession notice. Routine transmittal messages, meeting invitations, and comments on drafts would otherwise produce a substantially larger collection without adding a final playbook.

Until clarification is received, ORER will preserve the likely responsive final-version collections and begin identifying custodians. Search execution will use the clarified scope. The agency asks for a response by June 21, 2024. If Open Grid needs more time, the parties may agree on a revised date without closing the request.
