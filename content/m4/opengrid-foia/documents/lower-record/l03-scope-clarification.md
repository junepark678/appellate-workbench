## Agreed Scope Matrix

The agreed universe contains four categories. Category A consists of final Operational Decision Trees; Category B consists of final Cyber-Incident Escalation Matrices; Category C consists of final Response-Coordination Appendices; Category D consists of unique final approval, supersession, and distribution records. A final tabletop edition or finalized quality-review map is placed in the corresponding substantive category, not treated as a draft.

The date test asks whether an approved edition was effective at any time from January 1, 2022, through May 31, 2024. The copy test retains one authoritative record when duplicates contain the same content. The correspondence test retains only a final approval or supersession instrument. The exclusion test removes drafts, comments, meeting traffic, actual incident material, raw telemetry, system configurations, source code, credentials, real-infrastructure diagrams, and personal contact fields.

Searches will combine controlled fields with the terms Grid Sentinel, Operational Decision Tree, Cyber-Incident Escalation Matrix, Response-Coordination Appendix, revision approval, and supersession. Custodian review will decide finality and responsiveness after automated collection. Reviewers will separately consider withholding, foreseeable harm, and segregability; the scope agreement does not predetermine any exemption.

Both correspondents accept this matrix as the reproducible search boundary. The agency reference is CLR-OG-0621. All institutions, repositories, people, and program names in the exchange are fictional.
