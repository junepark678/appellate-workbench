Exemption 7(E) reaches law-enforcement techniques and procedures and also guidelines whose disclosure could reasonably be expected to risk circumvention. The agency need not show that a technique is entirely unknown; implementation detail may remain protected even when the general method is familiar.

Exemption 7(F) applies where disclosure could reasonably be expected to endanger an individual’s life or physical safety. Identification of a threatened person by name is not required. A category can satisfy the exemption if the agency explains how disclosure of that functional type creates the expected danger.

Segregability requires release of meaningful nonexempt portions. It does not require disclosure of fragments that would reveal an exempt relationship through placement or that would have no informational content after necessary removal.
