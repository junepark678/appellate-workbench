## Version Comparison and Segregability

Current and superseded records can remain sensitive within the assigned facts because comparing them may reveal which fictional condition, sequence, or role relationship changed. That observation does not mean an older page is automatically exempt. Reviewers evaluated whether the relevant relationship remained discernible and whether a nonoperational portion could be extracted.

The records contain administrative elements. A revision block may identify approval and effective date; a heading may name a general function; an instruction may concern document ownership rather than response action. I asked reviewers to distinguish those elements from condition values and branch relationships. Several administrative passages were released in the partial records, while others were retained where the team believed surrounding context supplied protected meaning.

For the three records withheld in full, I concluded during initial review that the organization was continuous enough that isolated fragments would either lack meaning or reveal structure. That conclusion was recorded at the document and page level, not as a prose explanation for each line.

My August 2024 review also left a traceable worksheet for any renewed assessment required in this action. The preserved notes identify the record, page, asserted field type, and disposition existing at that time. They do not predetermine whether another reviewer could separate additional administrative wording after considering a more specific challenge.
