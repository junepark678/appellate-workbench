# Day-31 Dismissal Order
## Timely invocation

On July 8, 2025, the court considers the respondent’s invocation of the petition deadline. The Board entered the final order on January 13, and the petition was received February 13. Excluding the order date, February 12 was day thirty and February 13 was day thirty-one. After Riley v. Bondi issued, the court directed the institutional respondent to state its position by July 7. The respondent filed its invocation motion on July 2, and the response period has now closed.

Section 1252(b)(1) is a nonjurisdictional claims-processing rule, but Riley does not make a timely party invocation optional. The respondent expressly invoked the rule within the period set by this court. No later agency order, tolling event, extension, reopening, alternate receipt date, or waiver appears in the papers. The court therefore enforces the statutory deadline on the undisputed chronology.

## Disposition

The institutional respondent’s motion is granted, and the petition is dismissed under Section 1252(b)(1). Dismissal follows timely invocation of a mandatory claims-processing rule.
