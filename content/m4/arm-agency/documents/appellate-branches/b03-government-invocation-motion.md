## Riley and enforcement

Riley rejected the view that Section 1252(b)(1) withdraws adjudicatory power whenever a petition arrives outside thirty days. The decision nevertheless distinguishes jurisdiction from a mandatory claim-processing instruction. The respondent invokes that instruction expressly and asks the court to apply it to the disclosed day-thirty-one receipt. Enforcement here would rest on party invocation, not sua sponte jurisdictional screening.

The respondent acted within the court’s first post-Riley response period and has not taken a merits position inconsistent with the requested dismissal in this docket. Administrative receipt of the petition, assignment of a docket number, and service of the show-cause order do not waive an objection the court specifically invited after intervening authority. The motion seeks no ruling about how Riley would apply if the respondent remained silent or affirmatively declined to press the rule.
