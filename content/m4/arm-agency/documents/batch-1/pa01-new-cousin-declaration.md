# February 3 arrival at the family residence

On February 3, 2025, I arrived at the family residence at approximately 6:35 p.m. to deliver food
to S.M. A dark utility vehicle and a gray municipal vehicle were parked nearby. I saw four people
inside the residence through the open entry: two wore Unit Four-style uniforms and two wore plain
work jackets.

One uniformed person told me to wait outside because a regional inquiry authorized a records
search. I did not see a warrant or written order. I could see open cabinets and boxes placed on
the floor. S.M. stood near the kitchen with another uniformed person.

I noted permit `HL-47` on the utility vehicle because S.M. had previously told me to record that
number if I saw it. I did not know its connection to any invoice at the time.

<!-- PAGE BREAK -->
# Statements and items sought during the search

From the entry I heard a uniformed person ask where “the annex clerk” kept the North Quay Lamps
copies. Another person asked whether A.R.M. planned to return for the family work box. S.M. said
A.R.M. was not in Kalyra and that no procurement pages were in the residence.

One plain-jacketed person carried the family tablet to the utility vehicle and returned without it.
I saw another person place the paper notebook in a clear bag. I cannot state whether either item
was later returned because I left before the search ended.

No person struck me or S.M. while I was present. One officer warned me not to interfere and wrote
my initials in a small book. I did not hear a threat of torture. I report the words and actions I
observed without assigning the speakers' legal status beyond their clothing and vehicle access.

# End of the event and immediate notes

At about 7:05 p.m., a uniformed person told me to leave the street. I walked to a nearby transit
stop and wrote the time, vehicles, permit, clothing, and phrases in a message draft on my own
device. I did not send the draft because I feared the account was monitored.

At 8:17, S.M. called from a borrowed device. S.M. said the people had left and taken the tablet,
notebook, and a box of A.R.M.'s employment papers. I did not personally see their departure or
inventory and therefore identify that information as S.M.'s report.

The next morning I copied my draft into a paper note and kept both versions. I did not contact a
Kalyrian agency because S.M. said the earlier complaint had returned to Unit Four. That reason is
based on S.M.'s explanation, not my firsthand observation of the 2023 routing.

Every event I describe occurred after January 13, 2025, the date of the BIA final order. I had not
prepared an earlier declaration. Neither A.R.M.'s April 2024 exhibit list nor
the September hearing receipt identifies me as a witness.

I first learned that counsel wanted my account on February 16, first spoke with counsel that day,
