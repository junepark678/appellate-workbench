# Charge and theory of removability

DHS states one charge: after admission as a nonimmigrant, A.R.M. remained for a time longer than
permitted. The charge rests on the alleged December 31, 2023 expiration and January 9, 2024
continued presence.

The notice separates that threshold charge from protection. Even if removability is later
conceded, A.R.M. may present the CAT application described in the scheduled proceedings. The
notice therefore does not state that removal to Kalyra will occur regardless of protection and
does not resolve whether withholding or deferral is required.

issuance record, and service materials. Procurement records, declarations, medical materials,

The notice uses a narrative format and carries no form barcode, street address, or personal
identifier beyond A.R.M.'s initials. Its operative content is the numbered allegations, charge,
