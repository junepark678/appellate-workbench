# Relief election and CAT-only scope

Counsel elects protection under CAT implementing regulations and requests permission to file an
application with declarations, medical evidence, translations, and country material. Counsel
states that the requested form of protection may include withholding or, if legally applicable,
deferral; the merits evidence will address the same more-likely-than-not torture question.

A.R.M. does not apply for asylum in this proceeding. The omission prevents the
record from silently converting a CAT issue into a protected-ground or discretionary asylum
case. A.R.M. likewise does not pursue cancellation, adjustment, voluntary departure, or a waiver.

DHS reserves the right to challenge the probability of future harm, whether predicted conduct
meets the regulatory torture definition, and whether any public official would consent or
acquiesce. DHS does not object to setting the CAT application for adjudication.

The judge records “CAT-only merits” as the contested phase and directs that future briefs keep
the theories separate from conceded removability.

# Country designation and removal destination

The judge asks whether A.R.M. wishes to designate a country of removal. Counsel declines to
designate any country and objects to removal to Kalyra on CAT grounds. DHS identifies Kalyra as
the proposed destination based on the conceded citizenship.

The judge designates Kalyra for purposes of continuing the proceeding while preserving the CAT
objection. No alternative destination is litigated. The record therefore asks whether A.R.M. is
more likely than not to be tortured in Kalyra, not whether relocation to a third country is
available.

Counsel previews three asserted risk sources: Directorate Unit Four, Harbor League actors with
official access, and central registry personnel who flag returned procurement witnesses. DHS
states that each alleged source and the government's response remain disputed.

The judge does not assign probabilities or require each source independently to satisfy the CAT
threshold at this hearing. Those questions are reserved for the evidence and decision.
