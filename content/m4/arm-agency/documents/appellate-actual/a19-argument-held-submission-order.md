# Order Recording Argument Held and Submission
## Argument held on August 5

Counsel for A.R.M. and the institutional respondent appeared for oral argument on August 5, 2025 before the three-member panel assigned to this matter. Petitioner addressed the admission and transmission history of P-7, the exclusion of the post-order proffer, the agency’s separate treatment of the asserted risk sources, and the complaint-routing evidence. Respondent defended the agency’s explanation and agreed that the corrected AR1–AR238 set controls merits review.

The panel also asked both sides about Riley v. Bondi and the February 11 tender followed by the February 12 cure. Respondent confirmed that it sought no dismissal of the actual petition on timing grounds. No new evidence was offered or accepted during argument, and no member announced a merits result. The court records argument as completed and submits the petition for decision as of August 5. Briefs, the corrected administrative record, the record orders, and the oral presentation are taken under advisement.
