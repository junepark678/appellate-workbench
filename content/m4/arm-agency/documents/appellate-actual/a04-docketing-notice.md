# Docketing Notice
## Record and briefing sequence

The agency must transmit an index and certification identifying the material on which the Board order was based. Court filings will use a separate appellate pagination so that a motion, order, or brief cannot be mistaken for evidence admitted in the agency proceeding. At this early stage the clerk does not certify a final agency-page total, decide whether any exhibit is missing, or classify any later proffer that has not yet been filed.

If the parties identify an omission from the agency materials, Rule 16(b) provides a procedure for correction. A request to add genuinely new evidence presents a different question and requires its own ruling. Once the court has resolved those questions and the agency has completed any directed correction, a briefing order will state the operative record and the sequence for principal and reply briefs.
