# A.R.M. synthetic fact canon

All people, places, identifiers, and record facts below are fictional and fixed for this
exercise. The Republic of Kalyra is an invented sovereign. No proposition about Kalyra is a
claim about a real country.

## Actors and places

- A.R.M.: anonymized petitioner; formerly a municipal procurement-records clerk in Orin Vale,
  Kalyra. No expanded name or unnecessary personal identifier exists in the exercise.
- S.M.: A.R.M.'s older sibling and a corroborating declarant.
- T.R.: A.R.M.'s cousin and author of the post-order extra-record declaration.
- Nadia Pel: fictional former Orin Vale municipal compliance officer whose authentication
  statement is a component of the family corroboration packet.
- Directorate Unit Four: fictional municipal security unit implicated in detention and abuse.
- Harbor League: fictional contractor network that threatened A.R.M. after procurement records
  were disclosed.
- Office of Civic Integrity: fictional Kalyrian complaint body whose intake protocol routes
  local-officer complaints back to the implicated regional command.
- Orin Vale, Merrow Province, and the Republic of Kalyra: wholly fictional locations.

## Fixed merits wedge

A.R.M. fears harm from Unit Four officers, Harbor League members acting with official access,
and central registry personnel who can identify returnees. The CAT analysis must consider the
combined risk rather than demand that any source alone cross the more-likely-than-not threshold.
Official acquiescence evidence includes the prior detention, the complaint-routing protocol,
Pel's authenticated account of supervisors returning complaints to implicated units, and evidence
about the absence of independent protective action. Formal anti-torture rules are part of the
record but do not answer willful blindness by themselves.

The authored disposition grants record correction, denies supplementation, rejects dismissal of
the actual day-29 petition, grants the petition in part, vacates the BIA order for aggregate-risk
and official-acquiescence errors, remands, and denies a direct appellate CAT award. Seven
production-engine journals cover the actual path and six adverse paths. The exact root records
level 2 in all seven realism dimensions with `independent_review_pending`; it does not claim
qualified independent review or level 3.
