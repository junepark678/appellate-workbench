Shared FRAP 15, 17, 25, 26, 28, 31, 34, 36, 40, and 41 and Fourth Circuit local-procedure
authorities remain dependency-owned. In particular, Rule 40(d)(1), in the official rules PDF
effective December 1, 2025, supplies the 45-day rehearing period when a United States agency is
a party; Rule 41(b) supplies the mandate date seven days after the rehearing period expires,
subject to a timely rehearing petition, court order, or stay. Rule 26(a) controls calendar-day,
weekend, and holiday counting. The root does not copy or shadow those authorities.

## Synthetic source classes

Every source under `documents/batch-1/` was written for this exercise. Each page advances a
specific procedural or evidentiary fact. No page was scraped from a real person's immigration
file, declaration, medical record, agency charge, or docket. The page labels encode two distinct
universes: corrected administrative-record pages use `AR`; the new appellate proffer uses `PA`.
