# Deposition of Draven Pike — role and authority

Q. What was your Blue Cedar role in March 2025?

in force and made the final manager-selection decision.

Q. Who supplied the financial target?

A. Sable Finch. Human resources, led by Mira Solis, maintained the workbook. Naomi Voss supplied
manager information.

Q. Could someone else overrule your manager selection?

A. Finance could reject the arithmetic and human resources could flag a compliance concern, but
I approved the final employee choice.

# Savings target and scenario design

Q. What target did you receive?

A. $612,000 annualized payroll savings across the covered group.

Q. Did selecting Benton alone achieve that amount?

A. No. Her cost was one component with support-role and vacancy changes. Scenario B-7 projected
$618,400.

Q. Were there qualifying scenarios that retained her?

A. Yes, when paired with an archive-hosting expense consolidation and a different manager. I
rejected them because I believed they posed greater continuity, travel, or automation risk.

Q. So arithmetic did not compel Benton’s selection?

A. Not by itself. I exercised operating judgment among scenarios that could reach the target.
