# Deposition of Leora Benton — appearance and background

Q. Please state your position at Blue Cedar before March 24, 2025.

A. I was a senior compliance audit manager. I planned regulated-client audits, assigned field
staff, reviewed exception work, and coordinated remediation with clients.

Q. When did you begin?

A. In 2019. Naomi Voss supervised me during the period in this case.

Q. Is your testimony based on your recollection and the exhibits shown today?

A. Yes. I reviewed the opposition messages, charge materials, workbook, scorecards, and my
performance records before answering.

Q. Were you disciplined before the reduction in force?

A. No. My 2024 review called me a strong performer.

# Duties and performance measures

Q. What work did you perform that overlapped with the retained managers?

A. Each of us planned engagements, selected field teams, reviewed findings, and spoke with
clients about remediation. We had different portfolios, but we worked from the same manager job
description.

Q. What was remediation quality?

A. I chaired a monthly review of serious control exceptions. After I left, Calvin Keene received
the review queue and Ms. Voss chaired the meeting.

Q. How was your completion record?

A. Forty-seven of fifty audits were timely in 2024. Two delays were client document holds and one
was a staffing gap. The workbook gave me 27 of 30 points.

# Lead assignments and sex-based concern

Q. What led to your January complaint?

A. I compared the people recorded as leads with the people doing the planning and field work. On
Lantern, Hearth, and Juniper, I or another woman performed substantial lead work while a male
manager received the recorded designation.

Q. Why did that matter?

A. Lead totals affected reviews and advancement. If the label was assigned unevenly, the score
could preserve the same disparity.

Q. Did you ask Blue Cedar simply to make you lead?

A. No. I asked for an audit and a neutral assignment rule. I said the pattern appeared sex-based
and asked that the metric be paused.
