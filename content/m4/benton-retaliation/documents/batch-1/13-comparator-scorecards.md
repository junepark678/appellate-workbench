# Common rubric and evaluation window

The scorecards use 2024 source data and the February–March 2025 RIF calibration. The weighted
maximum is one hundred: completion 30, continuity 25, credentials 15, availability 15, and role
uniqueness 15. Higher values favor retention.

All four managers supervised regulated-client audits, assigned field staff, reviewed remediation
plans, and reported to Naomi Voss for calibration. Specialized work could affect a criterion but
did not remove an employee from the comparison group.

Each card displays imported and final values beside its cited source or exception. A cross-score
audit records whether a changed field names a source, an exception, or neither.
