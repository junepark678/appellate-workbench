terminated 5 occupants, leaving 16 filled positions, and eliminated both vacancies, leaving 0
vacant positions. Final covered headcount was therefore 16:
`23 total = 21 filled + 2 vacant`; `16 filled = 21 filled - 5 terminations`; and
`16 final positions = 23 total - 5 terminations - 2 vacancies`.

The final model projected $618,400 in annualized payroll and benefit savings, exceeding the

controls documentation lead ($88,800), client onboarding analyst ($82,400), and vendor coordination

short of target. The February 24 revision added that filled position. Manager alternatives could
