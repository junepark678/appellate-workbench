## Agreed legend and limits of agreement

The parties submit this chart on December 5, 2025 at the Court's direction. “Agreed event” means
the date or occurrence is not disputed for summary-judgment purposes. “Competing inference” means
the source is authentic but the parties disagree about what it proves. “Employer position” and
“Benton position” summarize advocacy rather than establish a fact. Document references identify
the record item and internal subject; they do not add page labels to the underlying filing.

The chart separates admitted merits material from the late Wynn declaration and related
procedure. The Court's December 1 order governs that boundary. A procedural reference identifies
what was offered, challenged, or decided; it does not convert excluded words into merits proof.
