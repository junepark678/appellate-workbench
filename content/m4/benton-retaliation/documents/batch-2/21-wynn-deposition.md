# Deposition of Tessa Wynn — appearance and work history

Q. Please state your position and the date of your testimony.

A. I am an audit manager at Blue Cedar Compliance, Inc. I am testifying on October 2, 2025. I
worked in the same operations group as Leora Benton, Omar Ibarra, and Calvin Keene during the
2025 reduction in force, and Naomi Voss supervised us.

Q. How long have you held that role?

A. I became an audit manager in 2021. My work includes planning regulated-client audits,
assigning field personnel, reviewing findings, coordinating remediation, and communicating with
clients during transitions. The four managers had different portfolios, but we used the same job
framework and shared a field-auditor pool.

Q. Did you make the final reduction-in-force decision?

A. No. I did not attend the calibration meetings and did not score Benton or any other manager.
I learned that I was retained after the decision was made. I can describe my work, records shown
to me, and conversations about assignments, but I cannot testify from personal knowledge about
everything Pike considered when he approved the final scenario.

# Duties shared with Benton

Q. What functions did you and Benton both perform?

Q. Could another manager receive a client transition?

A. Yes, with a handoff. It might require calls, file review, and extra hours, but reassignment was
possible. After Benton left, I took Northline while retaining Lantern. Ibarra took Oriole, and
Keene received the remediation queue.
