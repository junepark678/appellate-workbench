# JUDGMENT
## Disposition

Entered June 15, 2026.

In accordance with the opinion entered this date, the court AFFIRMS the district court's December
1, 2025 order insofar as it excluded the materially new subject in the late Wynn declaration while
preserving Wynn's timely disclosed comparator testimony. The court VACATES the December 19, 2025
summary judgment and final judgment on Benton's Title VII retaliation claim. The admitted record,
viewed in Benton's favor, presents genuine disputes of material fact concerning pretext and
but-for causation.

The case is REMANDED to the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Virginia for
proceedings consistent with the opinion. The court expresses no view on witness credibility or the
ultimate merits. Each party shall bear its own costs in this court because the judgment affords
mixed relief. The formal disposition is: AFFIRMED IN PART, VACATED IN PART, AND REMANDED.
