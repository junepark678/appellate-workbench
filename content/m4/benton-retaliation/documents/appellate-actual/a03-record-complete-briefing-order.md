# RECORD-COMPLETE AND BRIEFING ORDER
## Certified record accepted

The clerk has reviewed the district court's January 23, 2026 certificate and the transmitted
docket in Leora Benton v. Blue Cedar Compliance, Inc., No. SYN-CA4-26-CV-4101. The appellate
record is recorded complete as of February 20, 2026. It consists of the thirty-seven district
record documents identified in the certificate and the continuously labeled pages JA1 through
JA262. The parties reported no missing transmitted filing within the period stated in the
docketing notice.

This order confirms record receipt and advances the appeal to briefing. It does not admit excluded
material for a purpose the district court disallowed. The Wynn declaration at JA194–JA195 remains
available to review the December 1 exclusion ruling, while the merits appeal proceeds on the
evidence the district court treated as admitted. Any brief citing the declaration shall state the
purpose of the citation. No party may characterize record certification as a decision concerning
truth, weight, waiver, preservation, or the standard of review.

## Principal-brief schedule

Appellant's opening brief is due March 16, 2026. Appellee's response brief is due April 15, 2026,
thirty days after the opening brief date. An optional reply brief may be filed within the time
allowed by Rule 31 unless appellant gives notice that no reply will be filed. A motion to extend a
briefing date must be filed before the affected deadline and must show good cause. The parties
shall not rely on informal agreement to change a court deadline.

Each principal brief shall identify the issues presented, state the applicable standards of
review, cite the exact JA pages supporting material factual assertions, and distinguish the
admitted merits record from material preserved only for review of the exclusion ruling. The
opening and response briefs shall address both the Rule 37 ruling and the summary-judgment ruling.
The clerk will record briefing complete only after both required principal briefs are accepted and
the status of any reply is known.

## Submission and service

After briefing is complete, the court will determine whether to schedule oral argument or submit
the appeal on the briefs under Rule 34. No argument date is set by this order. A later calendar
notice, if entered, will identify the date, place, time allocation, and any additional directions.
The parties shall remain prepared to address jurisdiction, the distinction between review of an
exclusion ruling and use of excluded material on the merits, the Rule 56 inference standard, and
the relief that follows from each possible ruling.

Electronic filing constitutes service on registered counsel. Counsel shall use No.
SYN-CA4-26-CV-4101 and the full caption on all submissions. The clerk shall send this order to
counsel for Benton and Blue Cedar on February 20, 2026. By direction of the court, the record is
complete, the opened stage is closed, and briefing may proceed under the dates stated above.
