# ORAL-ARGUMENT CALENDAR NOTICE
## Calendar setting

Issued April 20, 2026.

Leora Benton v. Blue Cedar Compliance, Inc., No. SYN-CA4-26-CV-4101, is calendared for oral
argument on May 20, 2026, at 10:00 a.m. in Richmond, Virginia. The court has allotted twenty
minutes per side. Appellant may reserve up to two minutes for rebuttal by notifying the courtroom
deputy before argument begins. Counsel who will argue shall enter an appearance at least thirty
minutes before the session.

The panel requests focused discussion of two questions. First, what information about Tessa Wynn
was timely disclosed, what materially new subject appeared in the November declaration, and how
the Rule 37 factors apply to the actual cure and scheduling record. Second, whether the admitted
evidence, taken together and in Benton's favor, permits a reasonable finding of pretext and but-for
causation without relying on the excluded subject. The questions do not limit counsel's obligation
to address jurisdiction and the standards of review.
