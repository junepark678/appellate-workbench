UNITED STATES COURT OF APPEALS FOR THE FOURTH CIRCUIT

# ORDER RECORDING ARGUMENT HELD AND APPEAL SUBMITTED

Oral argument was held on May 20, 2026, in Leora Benton v. Blue Cedar Compliance, Inc., No.
SYN-CA4-26-CV-4101. Counsel for appellant and appellee appeared and used the allotted time. The
panel questioned counsel about the Rule 26 disclosure subjects, the October and November timing
record, the Rule 37 factors, Pike's knowledge, calibration changes, comparator treatment,
target-reaching alternatives, the sequence of explanations, and the distinction between suspicion
and a reasonable but-for inference.

The appeal is submitted for decision as of the conclusion of argument. The court takes no
additional evidence and requests no supplemental briefing. The certified JA1–JA262 record and the
accepted principal briefs remain the decisional materials. This order records the occurrence of
the scheduled argument and the procedural act of submission; it does not announce a ruling,
forecast a result, or modify the issues preserved by the parties.
