## Findings Concerning the Appeal Waiver

The court separately examined the appellate-waiver provision. Serrano knowingly and expressly waived appeal of the conviction and sentence on any ground, including under 18 U.S.C. § 3742, while the agreement expressly excluded a claim that the sentencing court completely denied him the personal opportunity to allocute required by Rule 32(i)(4)(A)(ii).

The court explained that an ordinary challenge to the advisory role adjustment falls within the broad waiver even if Serrano presents and preserves an objection at sentencing. Serrano accurately described the distinct exception: a claim based on complete denial of his personal opportunity to speak before sentence remains outside the waiver. He confirmed that the exception does not convert every allocution complaint or Guidelines dispute into a reserved appeal.

Arden certified that she had explained the target-specific structure. Ives agreed that the government accepted the same meaning and would not treat the express exception as surplusage. On the totality of the present record, the court finds knowing and intelligent assent to both the surrendered rights and the retained claim. This finding does not predict a later sentencing event or decide how any future claim would be resolved.
