## Appeal Waiver: Express Exception

**THE COURT:** Now explain the exception in your own words.

**THE DEFENDANT:** If the sentencing court completely fails to give me a personal chance to speak before sentence, the waiver does not bar that claim.

**THE COURT:** It also does not convert the role-enhancement issue into an allocution claim. The role issue remains within the broad waiver even though the allocution exception is outside it. Do you understand the target-specific separation?
