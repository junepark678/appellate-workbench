# Judgment — Enhancement Appeal Dismissed; Sentence Vacated and Remanded

**United States Court of Appeals for the Fourth Circuit**
**United States v. Mateo Serrano**
Appeal No. SYN-CA4-26-CR-4201
Document 41
Entered July 28, 2026

In accordance with the published opinion filed today, it is adjudged that Mateo Serrano's appellate target challenging the three-level U.S.S.G. § 3B1.1(b) adjustment is dismissed under the knowing and enforceable appeal waiver. This judgment does not affirm or reverse the adjustment on its merits and expresses no view about whether Serrano managed or supervised another participant.

It is further adjudged that Serrano's sentence is vacated because the district court completely denied him the personal opportunity to allocute before sentence and the plain error affected substantial rights. The matter is remanded to the United States District Court for the Middle District of North Carolina for full resentencing consistent with the opinion.

The conviction remains intact. The district court may calculate the advisory range anew, receive lawful submissions, resolve preserved sentencing questions, address Serrano personally, and impose an independent lawful sentence. No particular term or Guidelines result is directed.
