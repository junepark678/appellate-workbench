## Discretionary Correction and Remedy

A sentencing proceeding in which the judge never invites the defendant to speak lacks a safeguard the criminal rules treat as absolute. Leaving that complete denial uncorrected would diminish the fairness and public reputation of the process, particularly where the record supports a real prospect of a lower term.

The remedy is full resentencing.
