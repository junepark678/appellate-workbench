## Claims, Defenses, and Discovery Scope

Calder alleges retaliation under 31 U.S.C. § 3730(h) after she reported suspected federal-program billing discrepancies. Cinder Lake denies retaliation and asserts legitimate employment reasons. Neither side asks this report to decide the truth of the billing concerns.

Initial discovery will address Calder's reports, employment chronology, decision-makers, nonprivileged remediation facts, and ordinary billing-control records. Investigation communications may be withheld only on a stated privilege or work-product basis. The parties reserve relevance and proportionality objections to requests that sweep beyond those subjects.

## Preservation and Custodians

The parties will preserve relevant email, office documents, calendar entries, messaging exports, and ordinary compliance records from agreed custodians. Calder will preserve materials retained lawfully from her employment and post-employment communications. ## Privilege Review and Logging

A producing party may withhold an attorney-client communication or protected work product and identify the claim in a privilege log consistent with Rule 26(b)(5). The log should provide date, participants, document type, general subject, and asserted protection without disclosing the protected substance.

Categorical descriptions may be used for genuinely repetitive groups if they permit evaluation. The receiving party may request clarification of a particular entry. A challenge must identify the disputed item or category and explain why the stated basis is insufficient; disagreement does not itself authorize access to the withheld content.

## Proposed Rule 502(d) Protection

The parties jointly request an order under Federal Rule of Evidence 502(d) governing disclosure made in this federal litigation. Production under the order should not waive attorney-client privilege or work-product protection in this or another federal or state proceeding solely because protected material was produced.

The proposed protection is prospective. It does not determine whether Cinder Lake's August 21, 2025 pre-suit letter disclosed privileged information, and it neither erases nor creates any earlier waiver. The parties reserve their positions on that separate historical question.

## Clawback and Dispute Procedure

A producing party that identifies protected material in a litigation production will notify the recipient with enough information to locate the item and state the asserted protection. The recipient will sequester the item, stop use, and may present the claim to the Court without relying on the substance beyond what the rules permit.

Clawback under the proposed order will not concede relevance, privilege, work product, or waiver. The parties will confer before motion practice. If court review is necessary, they will use a public motion and appropriately handled supporting material rather than placing assertedly protected content in a public filing.
