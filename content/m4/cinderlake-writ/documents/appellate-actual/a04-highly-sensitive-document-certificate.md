## HSD classification

Counsel certifies that the petition and appendix contain ordinary sealed attorney-client and work-product material, not material designated as a highly sensitive document.

The HSD label is reserved for a separate handling category and is not justified merely because a filing contains privileged material. Here the ordinary sealing motion, public counterparts, confidentiality certificate, and controlled access list are adequate. The certification avoids using a more restrictive designation as shorthand for privilege and preserves the ordinary page-for-page public and sealed presentation.
