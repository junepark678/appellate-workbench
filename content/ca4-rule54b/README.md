## Authored result and alternatives

The actual district court dismisses Count I with prejudice on January 12, 2026, while Count II
and Copper Kestrel's $120,000 amended counterclaim remain unresolved. On February 4 the district
court grants Asterglen's Rule 54(b) motion and directs a separate partial judgment, but never
expressly determines that there is no just reason for delay. Asterglen files its notice on March
3, before the conditional March 6 thirty-day date. Timeliness cannot supply a missing final
decision, and the June 5 status certificate confirms that the two other matters remain unresolved.

The actual panel therefore dismisses for lack of appellate jurisdiction and remands. It does not
reach the Pilot License merits, does not affirm, reverse, or vacate the January 12 partial-summary-
judgment order, and expressly directs each side to bear its own appellate costs under FRAP
39(a)(1). The actual docket contains no rehearing petition or stay motion; the ordinary mandate
issues June 29.

The case resource fixes three structured disposition plans:

1. `ca4r54b.disposition.authored-dismissal`: dismiss the bare-certification appeal and remand;
2. `ca4r54b.disposition.counterfactual-supported-certification`: exercise jurisdiction and
   affirm the Count I judgment after an expressly supported certification; and
3. `ca4r54b.disposition.counterfactual-untimely-notice`: dismiss a notice filed after the
   statutory thirty-day period on an otherwise valid certification.

The two alternative plans are training inputs, not descriptions of the actual appeal. The frozen
workflow binds each of the three judgment operations to its accepted rendered PDF digest and
structured disposition plan; resolved-pack validation and evidence closure both pass.
