## Certified opinion, issuance, and return

The panel opinion accompanying this mandate holds that Rule 54(b) required the district court expressly to determine that there was no just reason for delay before its judgment on Count I could become final while other claims remained. The February 4 order granted Asterglen's motion and directed a separate judgment but omitted that determination. The separate judgment did not supply it. Because Count II and Copper's amended counterclaim remained unresolved through entry of appellate judgment, 28 U.S.C. § 1291 did not confer jurisdiction.

The opinion dismisses and remands without adjudicating the contract issue. It leaves the January 12 partial-summary-judgment order undisturbed and does not direct the district court to enter certification findings. The district court may conduct further proceedings within its own authority and consistent with the appellate judgment. The opinion and judgment are certified with this mandate as components of the Court's disposition.

The rehearing period expired June 22, 2026. No rehearing petition or stay motion was filed, and no order altered ordinary issuance. The Clerk released the mandate on June 29, seven days after the rehearing period ended. It is effective upon issuance under Federal Rule of Appellate Procedure 41(c).
