## July 22 Notice from Asterglen

To: Copper Kestrel contract operations
Copy: Meridian Silt integration delivery
Date and time: July 22, 2024, 8:20 a.m.
Subject: Immediate termination of BeaconRoute Pilot License and demand

Asterglen has completed its review of activity associated with the twenty-nine X-series environments. Asterglen concludes that Copper Kestrel used or permitted use beyond the twelve-terminal scope and outside the June 10 written load-test conditions. The unsigned June 3 proposal did not authorize that expansion, and Asterglen did not provide production approval on July 8.

Asterglen hereby disables the shared staging credential, terminates the April 15 Pilot License effective immediately, and demands $480,000 in contract damages. The demand consists of $348,000 in asserted license and contract value and $132,000 in response, containment, and forensic-review costs.

This notice supplies zero cure days. Asterglen considers the identified consequences already incurred and does not offer Copper Kestrel an opportunity to avoid termination or the damages demand by reducing the configuration. Copper Kestrel and Meridian Silt must preserve records and cease any new X-series activity.

Asterglen reserves all rights concerning continuing conduct and confidential information.
