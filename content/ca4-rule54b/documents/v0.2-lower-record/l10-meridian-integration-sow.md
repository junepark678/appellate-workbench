## Statement of Work MS-24-17

This Statement of Work is effective June 14, 2024, between Copper Kestrel Logistics, LLC, as Customer, and Meridian Silt Holdings, LLC, as Integrator.

Customer has asked Integrator to support evaluation of BeaconRoute connectors associated with a possible twenty-nine-terminal expansion. The parties understand that Asterglen Freight Software, Inc. controls the platform and is not a party to this Statement of Work. Customer will obtain and maintain any required software permission.

Integrator will perform mapping, configuration, test execution, results documentation, and transition assistance within credentials and instructions supplied for the project. Work for a proposed terminal is technical preparation unless Customer confirms that the relevant software provider has approved a later operational stage.

The existing BeaconRoute pilot covers twelve terminals. The additional group remains subject to a proposed change order and written load-test conditions. Nothing in this Statement of Work amends the Pilot License, grants a software right, or authorizes Integrator to disregard a restriction communicated by Asterglen.

The project begins with planning and may proceed through the milestones described below. Advancement depends on stated prerequisites rather than elapsed time alone.

## 1. Scope of Services

Integrator will collect endpoint descriptions for X-01 through X-29, compare them with Customer's proposed schedule, and prepare a mapping record for approval. It will not alter the twelve existing terminal entries except when a test plan expressly calls for baseline measurement.

After receiving restricted Identifiers, Integrator may configure connectors in an isolated state, validate authentication, confirm data fields, and document exceptions. It will label each environment according to status: proposed, mapped, configured, test-ready, under test, suspended, or closed.

Integrator may execute an approved load script during a monitored window. It will use the group size, traffic range, duration, and rollback instruction communicated for that window. A material deviation requires confirmation from Customer and, when it affects platform restrictions, from Asterglen.

Integrator will deliver daily status during active testing and a consolidated results package at milestone review. Reports will distinguish simulated or replayed information from any approved live-data sample.

If the proposed commercial expansion is not signed, Integrator will assist Customer in disabling proposed connectors and verifying terminal state. Closeout assistance is within scope even though it does not signify production approval.

Excluded services include purchasing licenses, negotiating Asterglen's fees, making legal interpretations for Customer, directing ordinary freight operations, or representing that a technical credential is a production entitlement.

<!-- PAGE BREAK -->

## 2. Roles and Dependencies

Copper Kestrel is responsible for project direction, accurate terminal information, operational contacts, test data, continuity planning, and confirmation that permissions are in place. Customer will provide Meridian Silt with the June 3 proposal, Asterglen's June 10 written conditions, and any later instruction that affects the work.

Meridian Silt is responsible for competent technical performance, access discipline, status records, exception reporting, and compliance with supplied test controls. Integrator will notify Customer if a requested task appears inconsistent with a written platform restriction.

Asterglen retains control of BeaconRoute provisioning, monitoring, platform capacity, and software permissions. References to Asterglen describe an external dependency and create no obligation under this Statement of Work.

Customer will designate an operations role empowered to approve ordinary project sequencing. Integrator will designate a delivery role responsible for the milestone record. Asterglen's designated operations channel will be used for matters requiring platform confirmation; no individual personal name is necessary for authorization under this document.

Dependencies include receipt of endpoint mappings, availability of restricted Identifiers, an agreed test window, representative data, and rollback access. A delayed dependency adjusts the associated milestone without authorizing work around the missing control.

Each party will preserve project communications and logs reasonably needed to establish what work occurred, when it occurred, and which direction applied.

## 3. Milestone Table

| Milestone | Work | Entry condition | Completion evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | Inventory and map twenty-nine proposed environments | Customer supplies endpoint list | Reconciled X-01 through X-29 mapping |
| M2 | Configure restricted connectors | Asterglen provisions test Identifiers | Status record showing no ordinary routing |
| M3 | Validate authentication and fields | M2 exceptions resolved | Connector checklist and exception log |
| M4 | Execute monitored load groups | Written window and script approved | Time-stamped group results and rollback check |
| M5 | Conduct readiness review | M4 package delivered | Joint issue list and recommendation |
| M6 | Transition or close proposed connectors | Signed expansion or closeout direction | Final state confirmation by environment |

Milestones are sequential unless the affected parties approve a safe overlap in writing. Completion of M1 through M4 establishes technical progress only. M5 requires the participants to review performance, outstanding restrictions, and commercial status. M6 has two possible paths: transition following a valid expansion approval, or closure if approval is absent.

The milestone record will state the evidence used and any unresolved issue. A connector does not advance simply because its credential exists, and no group advances from load testing to ordinary routing based solely on acceptable performance. Customer bears responsibility for obtaining the external authorization needed for the transition path.

## 4. Test Controls and Incident Handling

Meridian Silt will implement least-privilege access and will use separate credentials or traceable assignments for each proposed environment. It will not share authentication material with terminal personnel outside the approved project group.

Before a monitored run, Integrator will confirm group membership, volume, duration, data source, observation channel, and rollback step. The run record will show the actual start, any pause, adjustments, completion, and final connector status.

An unintended operational connection, unexplained traffic source, credential exposure, or request outside the approved script is an exception. Integrator will stop the affected activity when safe, preserve relevant information, and notify Copper Kestrel. Customer will engage Asterglen when the exception concerns platform access or written restrictions.

Integrator will not infer permission from an available credential, a successful connection, an unopposed monitoring event, or an oral comment that conflicts with written conditions. If a later oral instruction appears to clarify an ambiguous test mechanic, the delivery role will record the speaker's organizational role, the instruction, and the limited task affected, then seek written confirmation.

Shutdown work may include inventory, log collection, configuration freeze, connector disabling, and verification. Those steps are permitted after a dispute or termination only to the extent access remains available and the affected parties direct orderly closure.

## 5. Fees, Invoicing, and Change Requests

Copper Kestrel will pay Meridian Silt for professional services used to complete the milestones. Charges will be based on recorded effort and approved project expenses under the parties' service schedule. Meridian Silt will invoice by milestone period with a concise description of work, exceptions, and status.

This Statement of Work does not include any BeaconRoute license fee. The $348,000 estimate in Copper Kestrel's proposed change order concerns a possible arrangement with Asterglen and is neither compensation owed to Meridian Silt nor an agreed measure of damages.

Work materially outside the listed services requires a written project change signed by Copper Kestrel and Meridian Silt. A project change may adjust Integrator effort, delivery timing, or technical tasks, but it cannot modify Asterglen's Pilot License or approve a terminal for production.

Customer will review an invoice promptly and identify a disputed item with enough detail for reconciliation. It will pay undisputed charges under the service schedule. Meridian Silt will retain time and expense support reasonably sufficient for review.

If a dependency is delayed, the parties will adjust the affected estimate. Meridian Silt need not continue a task that would violate a supplied platform restriction, and a pause for that reason will be documented rather than treated as completion.

No projected fee under this document guarantees that the commercial expansion will occur.

## 6. Acceptance, Records, and Signatures

A milestone is accepted when Copper Kestrel confirms that the stated completion evidence has been delivered or fails to identify a specific deficiency within the agreed review interval. Acceptance of integration work does not constitute Asterglen's approval and does not change a terminal's license status.

Meridian Silt will retain mapping records, test logs, exception notices, status reports, and closeout confirmation created in the engagement. Customer may obtain copies for project administration, dispute review, or continuity planning, subject to reasonable confidentiality protections.

Each party will protect the other's nonpublic information and any Asterglen material supplied for the project. Upon completion, Meridian Silt will return or dispose of credentials as directed and preserve only copies required for ordinary record retention or a known dispute.

The parties may sign counterparts electronically. This Statement of Work, with referenced service terms, contains their agreement concerning the described integration services. It does not supersede the BeaconRoute Pilot License.
