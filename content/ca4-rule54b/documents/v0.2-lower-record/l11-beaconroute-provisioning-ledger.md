## Ledger Summary

System: BeaconRoute pilot environment
Customer: Copper Kestrel Logistics, LLC
Ledger period: April 15 through July 1, 2024

This ledger records technical Identifier provisioning for terminal environments associated with the Copper Kestrel pilot. It is compiled from credential issuance records, environment mappings, and status confirmations available through the close of July 1.

Total Identifiers provisioned: 41. Twelve entries, T-01 through T-12, correspond to the Licensed Terminals in the April 15 Pilot License. Twenty-nine entries, X-01 through X-29, correspond to the environments in Copper Kestrel's June 3 proposed change order.

The status “provisioned” means that administrative and technical steps created a system reference or credential in the Asterglen-controlled platform after a Copper Kestrel request. It does not state whether the connection was configured, whether traffic occurred, or whether a particular use was permitted. Those questions require the activity log and governing instructions.

The twenty-nine X-series entries were created for technical evaluation subject to the written conditions issued June 10. The change proposal had no Asterglen acceptance recorded as of this ledger date.

## Provisioning History and Control Notes

April 15: Pilot License effective for twelve terminal environments. No credential event posted on the signing date.

April 16–23: The platform provisioning process created BR-A01 through BR-A12 and associated them with T-01 through T-12. The pilot inventory then reconciled to Schedule A.

June 3: Copper Kestrel submitted Change Request CK-01 seeking twenty-nine additions. The proposal entered commercial and technical review; no acceptance was posted.

June 10: Asterglen issued load-test conditions. Copper Kestrel acknowledged that the proposal remained unsigned and that provisional credentials were not production approval.

June 18–28: Asterglen issued BR-B01 through BR-B27 in controlled batches after receiving mapping information. The entries were designated proposed/test.

July 1: The provisioning process created BR-B28 and BR-B29. Cumulative totals became twelve licensed-series Identifiers and twenty-nine proposed-series Identifiers, for forty-one overall.

Control note: the ledger contains no field that independently changes the Pilot License. Contract scope is maintained in the signed schedule and completed change orders. Provisioning staff may implement a test request after written technical approval, but the commercial-status field remains proposed until authorized representatives complete the change process.

No production effective date was entered for an X-series record through July 1.
