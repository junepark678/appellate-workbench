# United States District Court for the Northern District of West Virginia

## Asterglen Freight Software, Inc. v. Copper Kestrel Logistics, LLC and Meridian Silt Holdings, LLC

# Order Granting Rule 54(b) Motion

Civil Action No. SYN-NDWV-25-CV-0618
District ECF No. 60 — Entered February 4, 2026

The Court has reviewed Asterglen Freight Software, Inc.'s Motion for Entry of Partial Final Judgment Under Rule 54(b), Defendants' opposition, Asterglen's reply, and the existing record. The motion is GRANTED.

The Clerk is DIRECTED to enter a separate judgment in favor of Copper Kestrel Logistics, LLC and against Asterglen Freight Software, Inc. on Count I of the Complaint, consistent with the Court's January 12, 2026 memorandum opinion and order granting Copper's motion for partial summary judgment. Count I is dismissed with prejudice.

Count II of the Complaint against Meridian Silt Holdings, LLC and Copper Kestrel Logistics, LLC's amended counterclaim against Asterglen remain pending. This order does not adjudicate either remaining claim, fix damages on either remaining claim, resolve Copper's requested $120,000 recovery, or stay further district-court proceedings. All scheduling directions previously entered remain in effect unless modified by another order.

The Clerk shall transmit notice of this order to counsel of record and shall maintain the matter on the active civil docket.

It is so ORDERED this 4th day of February, 2026.

/s/ United States District Judge
