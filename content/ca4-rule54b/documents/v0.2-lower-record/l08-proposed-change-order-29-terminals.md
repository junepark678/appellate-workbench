## Change Request CK-01

Submitted by Copper Kestrel Logistics, LLC to Asterglen Freight Software, Inc. under the April 15, 2024 BeaconRoute Pilot License.

Copper Kestrel requests evaluation and, upon final approval, addition of twenty-nine terminal environments to the existing twelve-terminal pilot. The requested environments are listed in this proposal as X-01 through X-29. If approved, the combined licensed population would be forty-one terminals.

This document is a proposal for review. It identifies desired scope, technical assumptions, timing, and estimated commercial treatment so the parties can assess an expansion. It does not direct Asterglen to activate production service before written acceptance. Any preliminary credential or test environment supplied during review remains subject to Asterglen's written testing conditions and the existing Pilot License.

Requested evaluation start: June 10, 2024. Requested production readiness review: after successful load testing and reconciliation of terminal mappings. Proposed effective date for commercial expansion: to be inserted upon signature.

Copper Kestrel asks Asterglen to confirm capacity, required controls, and final pricing. A signed counterpart from each party is required before this request changes Schedule A.

## Proposed Implementation and Commercial Terms

Copper Kestrel proposes four stages. First, the parties would validate terminal mappings and create restricted credentials. Second, Copper Kestrel and its integrator would conduct isolated connector checks using nonoperational or controlled traffic. Third, Asterglen would supervise a measured load test under conditions it supplies in writing. Fourth, the parties would review results and decide whether to sign a production expansion.

No stage may be advanced merely because a prior stage generated satisfactory data. In particular, credential creation and load testing do not authorize ordinary freight routing at a Proposed Terminal. Copper Kestrel will maintain a status list showing whether each environment is mapped, configured, tested, suspended, or proposed for later production.

Estimated additional license fees: twenty-nine terminals at $12,000 each, totaling $348,000 for the contemplated license period, subject to negotiation and final approval. This estimate is not due under the current Pilot License and is not agreed liquidated damages. Implementation expense, taxes, capacity, and service levels would be resolved in the final writing.

Asterglen may propose capacity limits, monitoring requirements, or a smaller deployment group after technical review. Copper Kestrel may withdraw the request before signature. If the parties do not agree, temporary test access will be closed according to written instructions and the twelve-terminal pilot will remain the operative scope.
