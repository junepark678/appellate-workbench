It does not decide whether the proposed certiorari question was substantial, determine the merits of Count I, or alter the district court's authority over Count II and Copper Kestrel's counterclaim after remand. The July 10 order remains part of the post-judgment history, but it has no continuing force after entry of this order.

The clerk shall record Asterglen's notice and serve this dissolution order on all parties. The district clerk shall be informed that mandate will issue under the release direction stated on the following page.

## Immediate release following dissolution

The mandate is RELEASED for issuance today, September 15, 2026. The ordinary June 29 date passed while the timely stay request and later granted stay controlled the post-judgment posture. The July 10 grant was not a denial and did not begin a new seven-day period under Federal Rule of Appellate Procedure 41(b). No automatic date arose while that affirmative stay remained effective.

This order now ends the restraint and expressly directs present issuance. An additional seven-day delay is unnecessary because the Court is not denying the June 26 motion on September 15; it granted that motion in July and is dissolving relief after Asterglen abandoned the proposed petition. The clerk may therefore issue the mandate separately on the same date as this release.

The clerk shall transmit the June 8 opinion and judgment to the United States District Court for the Northern District of West Virginia together with the mandate. The appellate disposition remains dismissal and remand, and the judgment's costs provision remains in force. This release order adds no merits ruling and imposes no new condition on district proceedings. Electronic notice of dissolution and issuance shall be sent to counsel for Asterglen, Copper Kestrel, and Meridian Silt.
