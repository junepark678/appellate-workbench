# Fourth Circuit fictional/composite bench foundation

This directory authors the immutable schema-v2 pack
`foundation.us-ca4-fictional-bench@1.0.0`. The eight profiles are reusable interaction styles
for the M4 launch cases. They do not represent, imitate, or make claims about any real jurist.

Every identity is expressly `fictional_composite`, compatible only with an appellate court in
the `us.ca4` jurisdiction, and limited to observable simulation controls. Focus weights use only
the closed reusable `workbench.topic.*` vocabulary. The pack contains no case issue IDs, facts,
records, questions, authorities, outcomes, or dispositions.

The frozen profile shorthand from `docs/content/M4_CASE_MATRIX.md` is:

- Alder: clipped and direct.
- Rowan: technical, measured, and Socratic.
- Vale: plain and measured.
- Fen: expansive and hypothetical.
- Quill: clipped and plain.
- March: formal and technical.
- Reed: clipped and technical.
- Slate: measured and formal.
