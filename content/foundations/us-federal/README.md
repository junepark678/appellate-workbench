# United States federal foundation pack

This directory authors the immutable schema-v2 pack
`foundation.us-federal@2025.12.01`. It is shared procedure infrastructure for the Fourth
Circuit M4 case matrix, not legal advice or an independent legal review.

The pack owns only reusable federal material:

- a national appellate-calendar baseline for 2025 through 2027;
- selected Federal Rules of Appellate Procedure used by ordinary appeals, agency review, and
  original-writ workflows;
- selected Federal Rules of Civil Procedure used by the launch-case procedure families; and
- Federal Rule of Criminal Procedure 32 as the shared sentencing-procedure baseline.

It contains no case, party, record, factual proposition, issue, question, outcome, disposition,
or judge identity. Case-specific statutes and decisional authorities remain root-pack content.

## Provenance freeze

All entries were checked on `2026-08-11` against the official sources frozen by
`docs/content/M4_CASE_MATRIX.md`:

- Federal Rules of Appellate Procedure, effective `2025-12-01`:
  <https://www.uscourts.gov/sites/default/files/document/federal-rules-of-appellate-procedure.pdf>
- Federal Rules of Civil Procedure, effective `2025-12-01`:
  <https://www.uscourts.gov/sites/default/files/document/federal-rules-of-civil-procedure.pdf>
- Federal Rules of Criminal Procedure, official compilation dated `2025-12-01` (the official
  landing page reports that the Criminal Rules were last substantively amended in 2023):
  <https://www.uscourts.gov/sites/default/files/document/federal-rules-of-criminal-procedure.pdf>

The holiday dates are the reusable observed-date realization of the legal-holiday vocabulary in
Federal Rule of Appellate Procedure 26(a)(6). A case-specific closure or emergency order must be
authored by the case or jurisdiction pack and must not be inferred from this baseline.
